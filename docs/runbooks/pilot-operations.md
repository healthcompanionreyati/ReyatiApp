# Pilot operations readiness

## Named owners required

The pilot cannot launch until named people and backups are assigned for product, engineering, security, privacy, clinical safety, support, provider operations, database operations, communications, and incident command.

## Daily checks

- Authentication availability and abnormal failure rate.
- Appointment booking/cancellation success and idempotency conflicts.
- Provider verification and support queues.
- D1 errors, migration state, backup freshness, and restore-test currency.
- Privacy-safe log conformance and access-control denials.
- Any user safety report or emergency-routing confusion.

## Stop conditions

Pause the pilot for suspected unauthorized access, leakage, corrupted clinical records, inability to restore, broken role enforcement, uncontrolled outbound communications, or unavailable incident ownership.

Outbound email/SMS, independent authentication, partner programmes, payments, and review moderation remain disabled until their documented gates are approved and tested.
