# Incident response runbook

Status: Pilot foundation; accountable names and rota are still required.

1. Declare severity and record the UTC start time, capability, environment, and reporter. Do not place personal or clinical data in the incident channel.
2. Contain safely: disable the affected capability, revoke compromised credentials, or isolate the integration. Preserve audit evidence.
3. Assess exposure using identifiers stored in protected systems, never copied into general logs or chat.
4. Notify product, security, privacy, clinical-safety, and communications owners under the approved severity matrix.
5. Restore from a validated clean state and verify authorization, integrity, and negative-access tests.
6. Complete a blameless review with cause, impact, timeline, corrective actions, owners, and due dates.

Emergency care must never be routed through Reyati support. User-facing surfaces continue to direct life-threatening emergencies in Qatar to 999.
