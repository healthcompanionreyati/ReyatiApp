# ADR-016: Server-enforced controlled pilot scope

## Status

Accepted for implementation. Pilot activation remains blocked.

## Decision

The first pilot plan is restricted to one active organization and one named clinic or department, with 3–5 providers, 50–100 invited patients, and a 42–56 day window. Invitation-only access is mandatory and public registration is always disabled.

Plans require independent approval. Approval confirms the bounded plan only; activation separately revalidates the organization and requires an approved go decision from the pilot readiness register. Blocked activation attempts are audited.

## Consequences

Reyati can prepare a credible controlled-pilot scope without opening enrollment or claiming launch readiness. Participant invitation and cohort operations can be added within these limits after the go/no-go gate is legitimately cleared.
